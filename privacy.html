1. Who we are

Piú Bello Ristorante e Pizzeria (“we,” “us,” “our”) is a family-owned Italian restaurant at 3138 SW Martin Downs Blvd, Palm City, FL 34990. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

This website is an informational menu and contact page. It does not offer customer accounts, online ordering, online payment, or a newsletter sign-up, so we collect very little through it.

If you have a question about this policy, call us at (772) 666-5632 or speak to a manager at the restaurant.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Order and reservation details — your name, phone number, the items you order, pickup or delivery address, requested time, and any notes you give us when you call or order in person.
  • Contact details — a phone number or email address if you choose to provide one when asking a question or reporting a problem with an order.
  • Payment information — handled at the counter or table by our card terminal and payment processor. We do not collect or store card numbers on this website, and we do not keep full card details in our records.
  • Event or large-party requests — the details you give us so we can staff and plan for your group.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider keeps standard server logs when a page is requested. Those logs can include your IP address, browser type and version, the device and operating system you use, the page you requested, and the date and time of the request. We use them to keep the site online and secure and to understand roughly how many people visit.

Information from other sources

If you order through a third-party delivery platform or leave a review on a review site, that company handles your information under its own privacy policy and may share the order details we need to prepare and hand off your food.

3. Cookies and third parties

This site does not set cookies of its own, and we do not currently run advertising, profiling, or analytics trackers on it. We do not use pixels to follow you across other websites.

Some parts of the page are loaded from other companies, and your browser contacts them directly to display the page:

  • Google Fonts — supplies the typefaces used on the site, and receives your IP address and browser details as part of that request.
  • Framer — hosts some of the photographs and illustrations shown on the page and receives the same standard request information.

These providers may keep their own logs under their own policies. If we add analytics, online ordering, or similar features later, we will update this policy and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent first.

You can block or delete cookies and third-party requests in your browser settings. Because this site works without cookies, doing so should not stop you from reading any page.

4. How we use information

  • To take, prepare, and hand off your order, and to contact you about it.
  • To plan for larger parties and group bookings.
  • To answer questions, resolve complaints, and handle refunds or corrections.
  • To keep records required for tax, accounting, alcohol-service, and food-safety obligations.
  • To keep the website working, secure, and free of abuse.
  • To meet legal obligations and enforce our Terms & Conditions.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it to build advertising profiles.

5. When we share information

We share information only as far as needed to run the restaurant and the site:

  • Service providers — payment processing, website hosting, delivery and takeout platforms, and bookkeeping, each limited to what their job requires.
  • Delivery partners — the name, address, phone number, and order details needed to deliver your food.
  • Legal and safety — when we believe in good faith that disclosure is required by law, a lawful request, or a court order, or is necessary to protect someone’s safety or our legal rights.
  • Business changes — if the restaurant is sold or reorganized, customer records may transfer as part of that transaction, under this policy or one no less protective.

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

6. How long we keep it

We keep order and reservation records only as long as we need them for service, accounting, and legal requirements, then delete or anonymize them. Server logs are kept for a short period by our hosting provider. If you ask us to delete information and we are not required to keep it, we will delete it or tell you why we cannot.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold, including limiting access to staff who need it and using reputable payment and hosting providers. No website or record system is completely secure, though, and we cannot promise absolute security. Please do not send card numbers, passwords, or other sensitive details by text or through this site.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you and receive a copy.
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong.
  • Ask us to delete information we are not required to keep.
  • Ask us to stop sending you marketing messages, if we ever send any.
  • Not be treated differently for exercising these rights.

To make a request, call (772) 666-5632 or ask for a manager at the restaurant. We may ask a few questions to confirm the request relates to you before we act on it. We will respond within the time the applicable law allows.

Florida residents have additional rights under state law where it applies to a business of our size; we honor the requests above regardless of whether a particular statute covers us. We do not knowingly sell or share personal information of anyone under 16.

9. Children

This site is meant for adults making dining decisions. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them online. If you believe a child has given us information, call us and we will delete it.

10. Other websites

Our site links to outside services such as Google Maps, delivery platforms, and social media. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and what they do with your information is up to them. Please read their policies before sharing anything there.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the restaurant or the site changes. When we do, we will post the new version here and change the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we handle information we already hold, we will take reasonable steps to let affected customers know.
