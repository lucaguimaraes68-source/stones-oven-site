8. Allergens and dietary needs

Our kitchen prepares pizza, wings, burgers, tacos, and fried foods in a shared space using wheat flour, dairy, eggs, soy, nuts, and seafood ingredients. We cannot guarantee any item is free from a particular allergen, and airborne flour is present in a working pizza kitchen.

  • Gluten-sensitive crust. Our cauliflower crust is made for guests who prefer less gluten, not for celiac disease or a gluten allergy. It is prepared and baked in the same kitchen, with the same utensils and oven, as our wheat-based pizzas.
  • Vegan and other diets. We serve a range of comfort food, but choices for strict vegan dining are limited. Ask before ordering so we can tell you honestly what we can do.
  • If you have a serious allergy, please call ahead at (305) 664-1404 and speak to a manager before ordering. The decision to order remains yours.
